LIVO Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 2026

LIVO is an offline-first life management application. Your privacy is important to us.

Information Stored

LIVO stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Budget records
  • Income and expense transactions
  • Savings goals
  • Card nicknames
  • Billing dates
  • Due dates
  • Credit limits
  • Application settings

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Credit card numbers
  • CVV or security codes
  • Bank account credentials
  • Passwords
  • Government IDs
  • Sensitive financial information

Offline First

LIVO works without an internet connection. Your data remains on your device unless you choose to use future cloud backup features.

Third-Party Services

Version 1.0 does not require user accounts and does not transmit your personal information to external servers.

Data Security

We are committed to protecting your data and continuously improving the security of the application.

Your Rights

You may remove all stored information by clearing the app data or uninstalling the application.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
